# Privacy Policy — Swipe Sweeper

**Last updated: October 12, 2026**

Swipe Sweeper ("the App", "we", "our") is a grocery shopping list application that helps users organize shopping, add products using AI-powered features, and manage available AI usage tokens.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the App, why it is processed, and your privacy choices.

## 1. Information We Process

Depending on how you use the App, the following information may be processed:

**Account information:** The App uses Firebase Authentication, including anonymous authentication. If you choose to link a Google account, authentication-related account information may also be processed.

**Shopping list information:** The App allows you to create and manage grocery lists. Shopping list information is stored locally on your device as part of the App's core functionality.

**AI feature inputs:** When you use Voice Add, Home Scan, or Food Scan, the relevant audio, images, or text may be processed to provide the requested functionality.

**Token and usage information:** We use Firebase services to manage AI token balances, reward credits, usage limits, and related transaction records.

**Technical information:** Authentication identifiers, App Check information, request metadata, error information, and service logs may be processed for security, reliability, and abuse prevention.

**Advertising information:** The App uses Google AdMob. Advertising services may process device identifiers and other information according to Google's policies and applicable consent requirements.

## 2. How We Use Information

We process information to:

- Provide and maintain grocery shopping list functionality.
- Process user-requested AI features.
- Manage AI tokens, rewards, and usage limits.
- Authenticate users and protect backend services.
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
- Diagnose technical issues and improve reliability.
- Display advertising, where available.

## 3. AI-Powered Features

Swipe Sweeper provides AI-assisted features, including Voice Add, Home Scan, and Food Scan.

When you activate these features, the relevant input may be sent through our backend services to Google Gemini for processing.

AI-generated results may be inaccurate. Users should review recognized products and quantities before relying on them.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information through AI inputs.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App uses services provided by Google, including:

- Firebase Authentication
- Cloud Firestore
- Cloud Functions
- Firebase App Check
- Google Gemini
- Google AdMob

These services may process information in accordance with their applicable privacy policies and service terms.

For additional information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Shopping lists are stored locally on your device as part of the App's current design.

Account identifiers, AI usage records, token balances, and reward-related records may be stored in Firebase backend systems.

We use technical measures such as authentication, application verification, and backend access controls to help protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the App's services, maintain token and reward records, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

You may contact us to request information about your data or to request deletion of personal information associated with your use of the App, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.
