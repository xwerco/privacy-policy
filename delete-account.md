# Swipe Sweeper — Account and Data Deletion

Swipe Sweeper allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open Swipe Sweeper.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Select **Delete Account**.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to submit or complete your deletion request.

## Request deletion by email

Alternatively, contact **extreme96@proton.me** with the subject **Swipe Sweeper — Account Deletion Request**.

We may request additional information to identify the account securely before processing the request. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive credentials.

## Data covered by deletion

Account deletion requests cover the Firebase authentication account and associated personal data, including AI token wallet and usage records, subject to applicable legal or security-related retention requirements.

Shopping lists stored locally on the device can be removed by deleting the application's local data or uninstalling the app.

Any information that must be retained will be limited to what is necessary for applicable legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes. Users may contact us for details about applicable retention periods.
