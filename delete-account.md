# Swipe Sweeper — Account and Data Deletion

Swipe Sweeper provides an in-app account deletion option and accepts account deletion requests by email.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open Swipe Sweeper.
2. Go to **Settings → Delete Account**.
3. Review the deletion information and confirm your request.
4. When the deletion service is available, the app initiates deletion of your Firebase Authentication account and associated user-specific backend records.

**Note:** The in-app deletion service is being finalized. Until it is deployed and verified, please use the email request method below.

## Request deletion by email

Send an email to **extreme96@proton.me** with the subject **Swipe Sweeper — Account Deletion Request**.

We may ask for information necessary to verify account ownership. Do not send passwords or authentication credentials.

## Data deleted

Account deletion covers the Firebase Authentication account and associated user-specific backend data, including the AI token wallet, relevant usage records, and applicable AI reservation records.

Shopping lists are stored locally on your device. You can remove local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

## Data that may be retained

Limited backend records, including deletion tombstones, advertising reward verification records, and aggregated security or operational records, may be retained where necessary for security, fraud prevention, accounting, or legal obligations.

Such records are not used to restore the deleted account.

For details about applicable retention periods or to request further deletion, contact **extreme96@proton.me**.
